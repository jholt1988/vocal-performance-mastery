WARM-UP LIBRARY · one-page cheat sheet

Twenty routines from Vocal Performance & Mastery, Ch. 7. Every routine assembles the same five jobs — knowing the jobs is what lets you cut one down on a short day.
  1. 1 · Align and breathe — Posture and appoggio, mostly silent. Sternum up, sideways inhale, ribcage buoyant as you phonate.
  2. 2 · SOVT reset — Lip trill, straw, or a voiced ng / m so the folds meet with less push. A reset, not a marathon.
  3. 3 · Range through the handoff — Glides and scales crossing TA and CT without hunting a break to dramatize.
  4. 4 · Style calibration — One gesture in tonight's actual idiom, at the volume and mouth shape the mic will hear.
  5. 5 · Unload — An easy hum or a speech-range glide, to land the voice back where it started.

The twenty routines

7aClassical~84 silent inhales · trill 3×20s · ng–ah octave · u/o scale · messa di voce→ Legato, not ring-hunting. On a mic, skip the ring hunt.
7bPop~7speech → hum ×2 · trill 2×15s · ah 1-3-5-3-1 · uh mix pass · hook on mic→ Breathiness is a colour, not the default.
7cRock / belt~7bounce + 4 breaths · trill → ya siren · hey/yeah call · æ 1-5-1→ Clean first. No distortion in the warm-up.
7dJazz~7spoken swing · doo/bah · call-and-response · back-phrase · hum→ Mostly spoken before it is pitched.
7eMusical theatre~8three passes: legit / mix / belt · speak then sing→ Three passes, not one blended “theatre sound.”
7fHip-hop~8mark breaths · whisper-rap · half speed · full speed once · sung hook→ Gasping mid-bar = add a mark, do not squeeze.
7gFoundation ten-minute10align 90s · SOVT 2m · range glide 2m · vowel scale 2m · style 90s · unload 45s→ The default. This one earns all the others.
7hSOVT ladder10–12straw silent · straw 1 pitch · straw glides · ng hum · m/n hums · lip trill→ Climb only as far as the voice allows today.
7iRegistration / mix~11.5trill across handoff · ng–ah · uh arpeggio · sigh-to-call · o vs æ · one phrase→ Mix is a balance you adjust, not a place you find.
7jRange edges10recheck edges · low sighs · high straw/ng · vowel migration · +1 semitone · return→ Never push a note that is not there today.
7kAgility / runs~9.5trill staccato · ng staccato · triplet turns · sixteenth run · turn figure · one lick→ Precision first, then speed, then volume.
7lDiction10speech · on one pitch · twisters · tongue · lips/teeth · text into song→ Clip, do not bite. The tongue works, not the throat.
7mMessa di voce9set pitch · hum swells ×3 · ah swells ×3 · watch the edges · crescendo ×3 · one phrase→ Soft-first. A forced swell is worse than a small clean one.
7nVibrato~10.5straight then free · hum into it · breath pulse · descending releases · sustain · in a phrase→ You coax it. You do not manufacture it.
7oEar / pitch~11.5ref & match · intervals · scale a cappella · chord-lock · melody · drone→ Recheck the breath before you recheck the ear.
7pBelt preparation10bounce + breaths · speech-bright trill · hey/yeah · æ 1-5-1 · mix arpeggio · one belt note→ One clean belt note beats ten shouted ones.
7qFalsetto / head~10.5h onset · descending oo · ng falsetto · connect down · articulate up · one phrase→ Quiet enough that a neighbour would not hear it.
7rPre-show6align 60s · SOVT 60s · range touch 60s · style gesture 90s · text pass 60s · unload 30s→ Reminds the voice where it lives. Should feel too easy.
7sCool-down~5speech glide · hum decrescendo · vowel slide · speech range · quiet→ Optional. A vocal nap does more than any glide.
7tDay-after recovery~6assess · reset light · speech hums · one glide · no style work · rest→ Tired, not injured. If it is a problem, this is the wrong routine.
STOP if anything hurts. Pain, a note that vanishes, or hoarseness that persists are not a warm-up problem — rest, and see an ENT (Ch. 33). Keep pre-show work to 10–15 minutes: more than that spends the voice you needed for the set (Ch. 6).
